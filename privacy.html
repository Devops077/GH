Privacy Policy

Personal Health Data Sync (the "App") is a private tool used by a single person, its owner, to back up the owner's own health and activity data into the owner's own Google Sheet.

Data the App accesses

With the owner's explicit consent, the App reads, through the Google Health API and read-only scopes: daily steps, floors, distance, calories, active zone minutes, sleep duration, resting heart rate and body weight.

How the data is used

The data is used only to produce daily rows in a private Google Sheet owned by the same person. It is not used for advertising, profiling, analytics or machine-learning training.

Storage and sharing

Data is stored only in that Google Sheet and in local files on the owner's own computer and private server, protected by access keys. It is not sold, transferred to or shared with any third party.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

The owner can delete the data at any time by deleting the Sheet and local files, and can revoke the App's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions go to the developer contact email listed on the App's Google Cloud consent screen.
