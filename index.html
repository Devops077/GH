Personal Health Data Sync

A private, single-user script that reads the owner's own activity, sleep and body-metric data from the Google Health API and writes daily totals into the owner's personal Google Sheet for self-tracking.

The app is not offered to the public, has no other users, and does not collect, sell or share anyone's data.
